The file is checked against its md5.txt and the device's hardware version, written to the inactive slot only, and read back to confirm it. The slot you are running and the default slot are never overwritten, so a failed or interrupted flash cannot stop the device booting. Afterwards, choose how to boot the new slot on Boot Partitions. The tar needs RAM for itself plus its largest image while flashing; very large files may be refused.
