Router PIN
The PIN a client types in to join with its “WPS PIN” option (start a router-PIN window on a band above first).
A PIN is 4 digits, or 8 digits whose last digit is a checksum (Generate makes a valid one). Saving restarts the radios that have WPS on, so connected clients drop for a few seconds.
Push-button is the safer method: it only lets a device join during a short window you open here. PIN methods can be brute-forced, so turn WPS off on a band you don't use it on. Turning WPS on or off restarts only that band's radio.
